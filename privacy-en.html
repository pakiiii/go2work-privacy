2. Data We Collect

Depending on how the application is used, we may process the following data:

  • full name,
  • email address,
  • information required to log in and register a user account,
  • profile photo, if voluntarily uploaded by the user,
  • information about published rides,
  • departure point, destination, ride date and time,
  • bookings and booking-related information,
  • messages exchanged between users through the application,
  • user ratings and reviews,
  • information required to send push notifications,
  • location data when a user uses map and route-related features,
  • technical data required for security and proper operation of the application.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

We process data in order to:

  • register and manage user accounts,
  • enable users to publish and search for rides,
  • enable ride bookings,
  • facilitate communication between drivers and passengers,
  • display routes, locations and destinations,
  • send notifications relating to the use of the application,
  • prevent misuse and improve security,
  • maintain and improve the application's functionality.

4. Location Data

Go2Work uses location and address information to provide features related to departure points, destinations, maps and routes.

Location data is used solely to provide the application's functionality and is not sold to third parties.

5. Sharing Data with Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate certain application features, including:

  • Google Maps Platform – for maps, addresses and route calculation,
  • Google Firebase – for push notifications and related technical features,
  • Cloudinary – for storing users' profile photos,
  • infrastructure providers required to operate the application's server-side systems.

These providers may access only the data necessary to provide their services, in accordance with their privacy policies and applicable laws.

Go2Work does not sell users' personal data.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed where processing is necessary to provide the service, perform a contractual relationship with a user, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate interests, or where the user has given appropriate consent.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained while the user has an active account or for as long as it is needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

When a user requests account deletion, the account is immediately deactivated and scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days.

During this 30-day period, data associated with the account remains stored so that the user may restore the account if they change their mind by logging in to the application again and selecting Restore Account.

If the user does not restore the account within that period, personal data associated with the account is deleted or anonymized after 30 days, except where further retention is legally required.

8. User Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their accounts at any time directly through the Go2Work application.

After the request is confirmed, the account is immediately deactivated, and permanent deletion is scheduled for 30 days after the request.

During this period, the user cannot log in to the deactivated account normally, but the application may offer an option to restore the account.

If the user selects Restore Account, the deletion request is cancelled and the account is reactivated.

If the account is not restored, data associated with the account is permanently deleted or removed after 30 days, including profile data, published rides, bookings, messages, conversations, ratings, profile photos, and device and push-notification-related data, except information that must be retained to comply with legal obligations.

9. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or unauthorized disclosure.

However, no system for transmitting or storing data over the internet can guarantee absolute security.
