Deleting Your Go2Work Account

Users of the Go2Work app can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, open the Go2Work app and follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to your Go2Work account.
  2. Open your profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the account deletion request.

After confirmation, your account is immediately deactivated and scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days.

During this period, you can restore your account by signing in to the app again and selecting Restore Account. If the account is not restored, it will be permanently deleted after 30 days, along with the associated data, except for information we are legally required to retain.

What Data Is Deleted

After 30 days from the deletion request, Go2Work permanently deletes or removes data associated with the user account, including:

  • the user account and profile information,
  • the email address associated with the account,
  • the profile photo,
  • published rides,
  • bookings associated with the user,
  • messages and conversations associated with the account,
  • ratings associated with the user,
  • data related to push notifications and the user's device.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or for other legitimate and legally permitted reasons.

If any data must be retained, it will be kept only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose.

Problems Accessing Your Account

If you no longer have access to your Go2Work account or cannot use the in-app account deletion option, you can contact us by email:

gotowork.info@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your Go2Work account in your message so we can identify your account and process your request.
